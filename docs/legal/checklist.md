# Founder checklist

Not legal advice. Not a clearance. Bring this to a lawyer.

- Employment contracts for both founders: exclusivity, non-compete, IP in software, inventions, use of personal time and personal equipment. Re-read the consolidated texts with counsel rather than relying on a note.
- What pre-existing know-how is personal skill, and what employer method stays out.
- Written permission where the contract requires it.
- Do not approach an employer's customers or competitors until counsel says otherwise.
- Do not put this repo on an employer laptop, account, or GitHub org. Whether the machine used for this commit is personally owned was **NOT VERIFIED** by the implementation.
- Before any non-EU disclosure, ask counsel about Ley 53/2007 and Regulation (EU) 2021/821. Public certification texts are not a blanket clearance for technical data. Reading item: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-22437
- Read the copyright notice inside a regulatory PDF before committing quotations. This repo did not do that reading. No paragraph text was committed.
- Read the model license before distributing weights. No weights are in this repo.
- `docs/legal/gate.md` is a founder-directed start. It is not a record that counsel was consulted.

If counsel later forbids the activity, stop.
